CPOWER Announces 2026 Heavy-Duty Product Lineup

FEBRUARY 10, 2026

After rigorous R&D and field testing, CPOWER Tools is thrilled to officially launch our 2026 flagship product lineup. Engineered for professionals, this new series focuses on ergonomics, extreme durability, and job site efficiency.

Highlighting the release is our Professional PU Foam Gun, featuring a Teflon-coated barrel that completely eliminates adhesive buildup. Alongside it, our new Quick-Adjust Pump Pliers utilize an advanced push-button locking mechanism that dramatically reduces hand fatigue during heavy plumbing and HVAC applications.

These items are now available for bulk order and OEM customization. View our New Products Gallery for full technical specifications.
